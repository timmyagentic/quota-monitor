A few things worth knowing.

Is QuotaMonitor free?

Yes. QuotaMonitor is free and open source under the MIT license. Provider subscriptions and API usage are separate.

Are the cost estimates my actual bill?

No. API-equivalent estimates help you understand usage. They are not subscription charges or a provider invoice.

Why do quota windows differ?

QuotaMonitor shows the windows the provider currently supplies. Missing windows are omitted instead of being filled with stale data.

Which Macs are supported?

QuotaMonitor requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. Intel Macs are not supported by the official release. The app is Developer ID signed and Apple notarized.
